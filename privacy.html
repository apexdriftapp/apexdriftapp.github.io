Privacy Policy for Apex Drift

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Apex Drift ("we," "our," or "the App") collects, uses, and shares information when you play our iOS game.

1. Information Collected by Third-Party Services

We do not require you to create a standalone account with us. However, Apex Drift integrates trusted third-party services that collect certain device and usage data to operate the game, display advertisements, and process purchases:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising): Used to serve in-game advertisements. AdMob may collect your device's Advertising Identifier (IDFA, subject to your Apple App Tracking Transparency consent), IP address, device type, OS version, ad interaction data, and crash diagnostics. Learn more at Google's Privacy & Terms.
  • Apple Game Center (Leaderboards): Used to record and display your longest-run scores on global leaderboards. Game Center data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple StoreKit (In-App Purchases): All In-App Purchases are processed securely by Apple. We never receive or store your payment card details.

2. How We Use Information

Information collected through these services is used solely to:

  • Provide core gameplay, track unlocks, and display Game Center leaderboards
  • Deliver and measure non-personalized or personalized advertisements
  • Verify and restore In-App Purchases
  • Monitor app stability and fix crashes

3. Your Privacy Choices & Tracking Controls

You can control ad tracking at any time on your iPhone or iPad by opening Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and toggling off tracking permissions for Apex Drift, or by resetting your Advertising Identifier.

4. Children's Privacy

Apex Drift is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Game progress stored locally on your device is deleted when you uninstall the App. You can manage or remove your Game Center profile data directly through your Apple ID and Game Center settings.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at: apexdrift27@gmail.com
